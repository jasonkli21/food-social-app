# Sample artwork

The images in [assets/food](../assets/food) were generated using the built-in ImageGen tool. The bundled prompts specified editorial 35 mm food photography with natural texture, warm directional light, and no logos or text:

- Tamari miso ramen, chili oil, pork belly, and soft eggs on a dark walnut counter, framed close from above in portrait orientation.
- A burrata and pepperoni Neapolitan pizza with a lifted slice, basil, and chili oil, photographed from above in warm daylight.
- Grilled al pastor tacos, pineapple, cilantro, and lime on terracotta over forest-green tile, photographed in afternoon light.
- Brioche French toast with berries, cultured cream, and maple syrup on an off-white plate beside a sunlit café window.

The sample reel pans across the ramen image.
